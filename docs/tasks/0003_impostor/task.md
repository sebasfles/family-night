# 0003 Impostor

## Goal

From the lobby the host configures and starts Impostor; every phone jumps to the game, each player sees only their own card, they argue in person and vote on their phones, the result reveals who fell and whether they were an impostor, rounds continue until civilians or impostors win (with the last-chance guess), points land on the night's scoreboard, and the host replays or returns everyone to the lobby.
With this the family can play a whole night; phase 1 of the product is done.

## Scope

- shared: `impostor/rules.ts` pure functions (impostor cap by player count, role assignment and turn order from an injected random source, vote tally and tie resolution, win evaluation, scoring) and their Vitest cases from the plan's Testing section; `domain.ts` and `api.ts` gain `ImpostorConfig` with its zod schema, `ImpostorPublic`, `ImpostorSecret`, `MatchPublic` as the discriminated union on `gameType`, and the request and response types of every endpoint below; `constants.ts` gains the defaults and the impostor table.
- api, platform part (`services/matches`): `POST /rooms/{code}/matches` (host, validates, dispatches by `gameType`, `MATCH_IN_PROGRESS`, `NOT_ENOUGH_PLAYERS`, `INVALID_CONFIG`), `GET /matches/{id}/me`, `POST /matches/{id}/abort`, `POST /rooms/{code}/lobby`, `POST /matches/{id}/rematch`; the room remembers the last config for rematch; `/leave` and `/kick` of a living participant call the game's elimination; `Matches` repo with ULID ids.
- api, impostor part (`services/impostor`): preparation (word outside `usedWordIds` with per-category reset, roles and order with `crypto`, secrets per player and the `#MATCH` item with five guess options), `PUT /matches/{id}/votes`, `POST .../close-voting`, `.../next-round`, `.../guess`, `.../skip-guess`, `GET /words/categories`; round in bodies and `STALE_ROUND`; closing the round only in the write that wins the version condition; scoring once at `FINISHED`; `MatchSecrets` (SK `playerId`) and `Votes` (SK `roundVoter`) repos.
- `api/src/data/impostor-words.json` generated by the om-developer in Spanish (Sebastian, 2026-10-17): 10 categories of about 25 words each with one clue per word and stable ids: Animales, Comida, Lugares, Objetos de la casa, Profesiones, Deportes, Películas y series, Marcas, Países y ciudades, Cosas de la familia; Sebastian reviews the JSON in the PR.
- web, lobby: game selector with Impostor active (Just One and Wavelength stay "Próximamente"); config panel editable by the host and read-only for others (impostor stepper capped by online players, random order, tie rule, category to impostor, last guess, category chips from `GET /words/categories`); the list of who will play; Iniciar disabled with "Faltan N jugadores" under 3; "Esperando a que el host inicie..." for members; server errors as toasts.
- web, `$code/impostor.tsx` and `games/impostor/`: header with round and impostor count; card hidden by default, toggled by tap, short vibration on reveal and on phase change; turn order with the first highlighted; voting list with checks and "Votaron N de M"; host Cerrar votación with confirmation when votes are missing; reveal with ranking, highlighted eliminated, "Era impostor" or "No era impostor", tie texts, host Siguiente ronda or "Ver resultado" when the match ended; last chance with five options for the guesser and "X está intentando adivinar..." for the rest, host Saltar when the guesser is offline; final screen with winner, word, impostors, elimination history and points, host Jugar otra vez and Volver al lobby, others "Esperando al host..."; Eliminado and Espectador variants without vote controls; host menu Terminar partida with confirmation.
  Navigation to and from the game is the `$code/route.tsx` effect of platform.
- Secret query `['match-secret', matchId]` with `staleTime: Infinity`; no optimistic updates on votes.
- Tests: shared rules (impostor table, seeded roles, single winner, tie SKIP and RANDOM, zero votes, civilian win, impostor parity win, guess right and wrong, living impostor leaves, several impostors across rounds); api integration (start validation, 20 concurrent votes close the round exactly once, STALE_ROUND, rematch picks an unused word, scoring, a live match snapshot serialized contains neither word nor clue, `/me` filtered to the caller); web Testing Library on card, voting, reveal and config panel.
- Screenshots at 390, 768 and 1280 px with mocked data of: lobby with the config panel (host and member), card hidden and shown (civilian, impostor), voting, reveal (eliminated impostor, eliminated civilian, tie), last chance (guesser and others), final (both winners), eliminated and spectator variants.
- Docs: `document-task` updates impostor `trd.md`, `database.md`, `ard.md` and platform `trd.md` for the match lifecycle.

## Out of scope

- Just One and Wavelength (phase 2).
- QR code; text, voice or video.
- Any Terraform change.

## Acceptance

1. With fewer than 3 online members Iniciar is disabled with "Faltan N jugadores"; the server refuses a start with too many impostors or no category with the Spanish message.
2. Starting moves every phone to the game within a second; a member who joins mid-match sees "Partida en curso, juegas en la siguiente" as Espectador.
3. Each player sees only their card; the room snapshot, the events and `/me` of another player never contain another player's role, the word or the clue (network inspection and automated test).
4. Votes show who voted, never for whom; voting closes by itself when every living player has voted, or when the host closes it.
5. The reveal ranks votes, marks the eliminated and whether they were an impostor; ties follow the configured rule with the matching text.
6. The match ends correctly in the three cases (civilians win, impostors win by parity, impostor steals with a right guess) and the host can skip an offline guesser.
7. A living player who leaves or is kicked counts as eliminated with their role revealed, and the win is re-evaluated; a last impostor who leaves gives civilians the win with no guess.
8. Jugar otra vez uses a word not seen that night (categories reset when exhausted); Volver al lobby moves every phone to the lobby and the scoreboard shows the points.
9. 20 simultaneous votes close the round exactly once (test); repeated taps answer `STALE_ROUND` and change nothing.
10. Every verification target passes; screenshots of every screen and state are in the workspace.

## Approach

- Module and layers touched: impostor in shared (rules, types), api (`services/impostor`, repos MatchSecrets and Votes, word bank) and web (`games/impostor`, `$code/impostor.tsx`); platform in api (`services/matches`, Matches repo, leave and kick hooks) and web (lobby game panel).
- Entities, endpoints, tables: Match, ImpostorPublic, ImpostorSecret, Vote; the eleven endpoints above; Matches, MatchSecrets, Votes, plus Rooms (`usedWordIds`, remembered config) and RoomMembers (`totalScore`).
- Rules first in shared with their tests, then api services, then web; one PR, rounds.
- Decisions:
  - `MatchPublic` as a discriminated union keyed by `gameType`: chosen over a generic `publicState` blob because the web narrows types per game and future games add a member, not a field.
  - The word bank ships in the API bundle as JSON: chosen over a DynamoDB table because it is read-only data versioned with the code.
  - Card toggle by tap, not hold (confirmed by Sebastian 2026-10-17): a hold is unreliable on mobile browsers.
  - Elimination by leave or kick lives in the impostor service and platform calls it by game type: chosen over platform knowing the game's rules because each game owns what a departure means.

## Context & decisions

Consolidated 2026-10-17 by the om-reviewer with Sebastian through the om-manager.

Decisions (Sebastian):

- Scope change against the plan's route table: every match route lives under the room, `/api/rooms/{code}/matches/{matchId}/{me,votes,close-voting,next-round,guess,skip-guess,abort,rematch}`. Reason: `Matches` is keyed by `roomCode` with no GSI (`infra/stacks/app/database.tf`) and Terraform is out of scope; the room in the path is also the membership check.
- Scope addition: host-only `PUT /api/rooms/{code}/config` stores the Impostor config on the room, enters the snapshot and publishes, so members see the host's settings live; it is the remembered config the lobby opens with. Rematch uses the finished match's own config.
- Word bank in Peruvian Spanish where it differs (palta, fresa, polo, combi), neutral Latin American otherwise.

Decisions (om-reviewer, accepted by Sebastian):

- Terminar partida sets `ABORTED`, no points, and `currentMatchId` null in one write: every phone returns to the lobby; no aborted screen.
- A living player who leaves or is kicked in `VOTING`: their vote and the votes cast for them are dropped, those voters vote again, then the all-voted check runs.
- Points go to the winning side's participants still members when the match finishes.
- Reveal host button: "Siguiente ronda" (goes on), "Última oportunidad" (guess follows), "Ver resultado" (ended); all are `next-round {round}`.
- `guess.options` stays public as the plan types it; no field marks the right one before the guess. The no-secret test covers snapshots and events in `VOTING` and `REVEAL`; the word appears only in `reveal` at `FINISHED`.
- Rematch whose config no longer fits the online count answers `INVALID_CONFIG`.
- Pre-merge evidence is tests and screenshots; the PR description carries a multi-phone production smoke checklist (Acceptance 2, 3 by network inspection, 6) Sebastian runs after merge.

Decisions (om-reviewer):

- `GET /api/words/categories` needs any valid identity, no room. Skip-guess is accepted from the host in `IMPOSTOR_GUESS`; the UI shows Saltar only when the guesser is offline. `/lobby` with a `PLAYING` match answers `MATCH_IN_PROGRESS`.
- Round 1 delivers `shared` and `api` only; web follows from round 2.

Constraints:

- One write path: every snapshot change (start, vote, close, next-round, guess, skip, abort, lobby, rematch, config, leave and kick elimination) goes through `api/src/services/room-state.ts`, room bump conditional on `version` plus the match write in one transaction; `changesSnapshot` must see the match and the config (`docs/modules/platform/ard.md`, version bump entry; `document-task` updates it).
- Start writes room, match and every `MatchSecrets` item in the same transaction; a published match always has its secrets.
- Roles, order, word and tie draws use crypto randomness in production (`docs/modules/impostor/ard.md`); `deps.random` is `Math.random` today and must not feed them.
- Secrets never in snapshot, event or log (`docs/TRD.md` Conventions); the guess body is never logged. PUT requests carry `x-amz-content-sha256` like POST.
- Current match `ttl` renews with the room (`docs/modules/platform/database.md`).

Review beyond the Pipeline:

- 20 concurrent votes: all answer 200, all counted, one close, one `REVEAL` publish, no `CONFLICT`.
- Word bank: 10 categories, at least 5 words each, unique stable ids, clue never contains the word, family friendly, Peruvian vocabulary.
- The `$code/route.tsx` navigation effect, spectator and eliminated views, and screenshots of every listed state at 390, 768 and 1280.

## om-developer notes

### Round 1

Did:
- shared: `impostor/rules.ts` pure rules (cap table, start checks, crypto-injectable shuffle, word pick with per-active-categories reset, guess options, start, vote checks, tally and tie, elimination, outcome, next round, guess, skip, departure, scores) with Vitest cases; `ImpostorConfig` and its zod schema, `ImpostorPublic`, `ImpostorSecret`, `MatchPublic`, `RoomSnapshot.currentMatch` and `configs`; request and response types; new error codes; Impostor defaults and caps in `constants.ts`.
- api: `Matches`, `MatchSecrets`, `Votes` repos; `room-state.ts` now loads the current match, takes an async decide, writes match, configs, `usedWordIds` and extra items in the version-conditional transaction, and renews the match `ttl` with the room; platform `services/matches` (start, config, lobby, abort, rematch, `/me`, departure hook from leave and kick); `services/impostor` (preparation, votes, close, next round, guess, skip, departure); routes under `/api/rooms/{code}/...` per Context, plus `GET /api/words/categories`; word bank of 10 categories by 25 words.
- web: only the snapshot fixtures gained `configs`, so the build stays green; the UI is round 2.

Decisions not in the plan:
- A vote is two steps: a plain put of the ballot keyed by round and voter (no snapshot change), then the room-state write that recomputes `votedPlayerIds` from the round's ballots and closes when everyone voted. Retries that find their ballot already reflected are no-ops, so 20 simultaneous votes converge with at most two conflicts per request (measured) and never reach `CONFLICT`. A late vote whose round closed answers 200 if the voter is in the closed round's `votedPlayerIds`, `STALE_ROUND` otherwise.
- `votedPlayerIds` keeps the closed round's voters during `REVEAL`, including the eliminated; it clears on the next round.
- `RoomSnapshot.configs` always carries an Impostor config; the server fills the default (all categories) until the host stores one.
- `usedWordIds` is a list, not a string set: no empty-set special case, uniqueness kept by the pick.
- `MatchSecrets` and `Votes` `ttl` is set once at write (24 h); only the room, the writer and the current match renew.
- New error code `NOT_GUESSER` (403) for a guess by someone else; `AppError` takes an optional Spanish message, used for the specific `INVALID_CONFIG` texts ("Con N jugadores puede haber como máximo M impostor(es)", "Elige al menos una categoría").
- A match id that exists but is not current answers `STALE_ROUND`; an unknown one `MATCH_NOT_FOUND`; rematch while playing `MATCH_IN_PROGRESS`; abort of a non playing match `STALE_ROUND`.
- Rematch answers 200 (the plan marks 201 only for the start); it leaves the room's remembered config untouched.
- Leave or kick in `REVEAL`: the would-be guesser leaving gives civilians the win; another living player is eliminated and the match finishes at once only if the outcome is already a win.
- Room test helpers moved from `rooms.test.ts` to `test/harness.ts` for reuse.
